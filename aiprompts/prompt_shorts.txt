1. HONEST CEFR ASSESSMENT — READ THE WHOLE TRANSCRIPT, THEN CLASSIFY. This is graded strictly.    THE SIX LEVELS — pick the one the recording MOSTLY sits at:

     A2 — common everyday vocabulary; past and future added; short sentences joined with and/but/because; familiar concrete topics (shopping, work, travel).
     B1 — everyday vocabulary plus some topic words; a range of tenses; some complex sentences (relative clauses, first conditional); familiar topics with simple opinions; normal pace.
     B2 — broad vocabulary including some abstract and mildly idiomatic language; full tense/aspect range, passives, conditionals; longer multi-clause sentences; abstract topics and argument; natural pace, some colloquialism.
     C1 — wide, precise vocabulary with idioms, collocations and nuance; complex embedded structures; abstract or specialised topics; meaning often implied; fast natural speech with connected-speech features.
     C2 — sophisticated, highly idiomatic or figurative language; dense complex structure; highly abstract, technical or nuanced ideas; native-speed delivery with cultural references, irony or humour.
